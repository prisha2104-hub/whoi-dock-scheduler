## Cross-check — `8YR Dock Summary`

This summary **cannot be used as a validation oracle for the schedule sheets**, for three reasons established from the workbook itself:

1. It reports on berths the schedule grids of those years do not contain. `Marsh Landing` appears in **no** annual sheet at all, and `North Finger Piers` rows first appear in **2014** — yet both carry 2006–2013 totals. Those two rows account for 3914 of the summary's berth-days.
2. 7 cells exceed the number of days in a year, which no per-berth occupied-day count can produce — e.g. North Pier West 2012 = 648, North Pier West 2013 = 470, North Pier East 2013 = 631. So its "days" are not per-berth occupied calendar days.
3. It stops at 2013 while the schedule runs to 2019, and its `Total Days` row is a live `=SUM()` over those same figures, so it is self-referential.
